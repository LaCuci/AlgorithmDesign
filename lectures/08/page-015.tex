\subsection{Exercise: Partition}
Consider the following problem:

\textbf{PARTITION}

\begin{notesproblem}
\text{\textbf{I:}} & \langle S\rangle\text{, }S\subset\mathbb N\text{, finite.}\\
\text{\textbf{Q:}} & \exists S_1,S_2\subset S\text{, }(S_1\cup S_2=S)\land(S_1\cap S_2=\varnothing)\text{:}\\
& \sum_{s\in S_1}s=\sum_{s\in S_2}s\ ?
\end{notesproblem}

Show that $\mathrm{PARTITION}\in\classNPH$.
