\section{Search and numerical problems}
\subsection{Exercise: From SUBSET SUM decision to search}
Assume that there exists a polynomial algorithm $A_{\mathrm{SS}}(\langle S,t\rangle)$ for SS:

\begin{notesproblem}
\text{\textbf{I:}} & \langle S,t\rangle:S\subseteq\mathbb N\text{ finite, }t\in\mathbb N\text{.}\\
\text{\textbf{Q:}} & \exists S'\subseteq S:\sum_{s\in S'}s=t\text{?}
\end{notesproblem}

Design a polynomial algorithm $A_{\mathrm{SUBSET}}(\langle S,t\rangle)$ which uses $A_{\mathrm{SS}}$ as a subroutine and, in case $\langle S,t\rangle\in\mathrm{SS}$, returns $S':\sum_{s\in S'}s=t$.
