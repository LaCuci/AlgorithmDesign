\sourceheading[Exercise: LONGEST PATH]{EXERCISE}
Consider the following problem:

\textbf{L-PATH}

\textbf{I:} $\langle G=(V,E),u,v,K\rangle$, $G=(V,E)$ undirected graph, $u,v\in V$, $1\leq K\leq|V|$.

\textbf{Q:} Is there a simple path from $u$ to $v$ of length $\geq K$?

(decision problem for LONGEST-PATH).

Prove that $\mathrm{HAMILTON}\polyred\mathrm{L\text{-}PATH}$ (thus $\mathrm{L\text{-}PATH}\in\classNPH$).
