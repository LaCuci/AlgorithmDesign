\subsection{Exercise: Longest path}
Consider the following problem:

\textbf{L-PATH}

\begin{notesproblem}
\text{\textbf{I:}} & \langle G=(V,E),u,v,K\rangle\text{,}\\
& \text{ }G=(V,E)\text{ undirected graph, }u,v\in V\text{, }1\leq K\leq|V|\text{.}\\
\text{\textbf{Q:}} & \text{Is there a simple path from }u\text{ to }v\text{ of length }\geq K\text{?}
\end{notesproblem}

(decision problem for LONGEST-PATH).

Prove that $\mathrm{HAMILTON}\polyred\mathrm{L\text{-}PATH}$ (thus $\mathrm{L\text{-}PATH}\in\classNPH$).
