\section{Recap: Weighted vertex cover}
Weighted Vertex Cover $\langle G,w\rangle$:
\begin{itemize}
\item determine VC $V^*$ of minimum weight $\sum_{v\in V^*}w(v)$;
\item FAIR PRICING extends the maximal matching algorithm (unweighted case);
\item LP rounding:
\end{itemize}
\begin{notesproblem}
&\min\sum_{v\in V}w(v)x_v\\
&\text{s.t.}\\
&x_u+x_v\geq1:\{u,v\}\in E\\
&0\leq x_u\leq1:u\in V
\end{notesproblem}
\[V'=\left\{v\in V:x_v^*\geq\frac12\right\}.\]
\subsection{LP duality and fair pricing}
DUAL: (ignore constraints $x_u\leq1$: REDUNDANT)
\begin{notesproblem}
&\max\sum_{e\in E}p_e\\
&\text{s.t.}\\
&\sum_{e\ni u}p_e\leq w(u)\qquad u\in V\\
&p_e\geq0\qquad e\in E
\end{notesproblem}
Linear relaxation of fair pricing!
