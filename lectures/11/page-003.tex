If $\langle G\rangle\in\mathrm{HAMILTON}$, then the cost of a hamiltonian cycle of $G$ under $c(\cdot)$ is exactly $|V|$ $\Rightarrow f(\langle G\rangle)=\langle G',c,|V|\rangle\in\mathrm{TSP}$.

If $f(\langle G\rangle)=\langle G,c,|V|\rangle\in\mathrm{TSP}$ then there is a tour $\gamma$ (made of $|V|$ edges) of cost $c(\gamma)=|V|$
\begin{align*}
&\Rightarrow\text{ALL edges in the tour must cost 1}\\
&\Rightarrow\text{ALL edges in the tour belong to $E$}\\
&\Rightarrow\text{the tour is a hamiltonian cycle of $G$}\\
&\Rightarrow\langle G\rangle\in\mathrm{HAMILTON}.
\end{align*}
Two interesting results on TSP:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Strong inapproximability result of general TSP: if $\classP\ne\classNP$ there cannot exist a poly-time $\rho(n)$-approximation for many functions $\rho(n)$.
\item Good approximation algorithms for a ``natural'' (still hard) restriction of TSP (metric cost function):
\[2\text{-approximation (based on MST)}\]
\[\tfrac32\text{-approximation (Christofides' algorithm)}\]
(based on MST and min-cost matching).
\end{enumerate}
