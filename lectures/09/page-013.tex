\subsection{Maximal matchings and lessons learned}
\textbf{LESSONS LEARNED:} To get a bound on the approximation we need to establish explicit bounds (lower or upper) on $C(s^*)$.

For VC, we establish such a (lower) bound by relating $|V^*|$ (cannot be computed) to the size of an easily obtainable construct (a matching $A$ of $G$).

We will see that this approach can be used for other problems.

\textbf{OBSERVATION:} The matching $A$ constructed implicitly by A-V-C $(G)$ is maximal, in the sense that $\forall e\in E:A\cup\{e\}$ is not a matching --- (PROVE IT!).

\textbf{EXERCISE:} Prove that the set of all nodes forming the edges of a maximal matching are a vertex cover.
