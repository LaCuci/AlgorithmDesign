\textbf{Morale:} While optimization problems associated to NPC problems are all equivalent w.r.t. exact solution, they behave differently w.r.t. approximation.

\textbf{Intuition:} The idea of reducibility can be extended to optimization problems using an extra function $g$, mapping each element of $\mathcal S(f(i))$ into an element of $\mathcal S(i)$:
\sourcefigure[0.9]{assets/09/page-008-optimization-reduction.png}
Function $g$ maps optimal solutions in $\mathcal S(f(i))$ into optimal solutions in $\mathcal S(i)$, however the mapping does not necessarily preserve the approximation quality!
