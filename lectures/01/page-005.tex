\subsection{Written Exam}
\textbf{Part 1: theory} questions on covered material (proofs, definitions, analyses): $30\%$.

\textbf{Part 2: creative exercises} apply techniques to new problems (lots of exercise sessions during lectures): $70\%$.

\textbf{Voluntary teamwork:} groups of 2 students. Read and understand advanced material, prepare a short written report plus class-presentation (with slides).

\textbf{Evaluation:} Exemption from Part 1 and $30\%$ of final grade.

\begin{itemize}
\item team creation + assignment: first weeks of december.
\item presentation must be done in January (last week of lectures), Jan 11--13.
\end{itemize}
