2. $\mathrm{SAT}\in\classNPH$. We show that $\mathrm{BC\text{-}SAT}\polyred\mathrm{SAT}$.

Reduction function $f$: must transform a circuit $C$ into a formula $\phi_C$:
\[
C\text{ is satisfiable}\Longleftrightarrow f(C)=\phi_C\text{ is satisfiable},
\]
\[
\langle C\rangle\in\mathrm{BC\text{-}SAT}\Longleftrightarrow\langle\phi_C\rangle=f(\langle C\rangle)\in\mathrm{SAT}.
\]
Seems easy: straightforward method: compute the subexpression associated to each wire. $\phi_C=$ expression associated to output wire.
