We have
\[
\begin{aligned}
x\in L&\Longleftrightarrow\exists\bar y,\ |\bar y|=O(|x|^{k_1}):\ V_L(x,\bar y)=1\\
&\Longleftrightarrow C_{V_L,x}(\bar y)=1\\
&\qquad\{C_{V_L,x}\text{ simulates }V_L(x,y)\}\\
&\Longleftrightarrow\langle C_{V_L,x}\rangle\in\mathrm{BC\text{-}SAT}\\
&\Longleftrightarrow f_{L\to\mathrm{BC\text{-}SAT}}(x)\in\mathrm{BC\text{-}SAT}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus $L\polyred\mathrm{BC\text{-}SAT}$.
This proves that $\mathrm{BC\text{-}SAT}\in\classNPH$.

In what follows we will prove that a number of problems are in NPC. The details of the reductions will teach us general approaches and highlight pitfalls to avoid.
