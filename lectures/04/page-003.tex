Wires that do not originate from the output of a gate are called inputs of the circuit. They are dangling wires. They are Level 0. 

Wires that do not enter into a gate are called outputs of the circuit.
We will mostly consider circuits with a single output.

FAN-IN: max number of wires entering a gate over all gates in the circuit. 

FAN-OUT: max number of gates fed by the same wire over all gates in the circuit.
