A circuit can be represented by a directed graph (circuits are in a 1 to 1 correspondence on a directed graph). 
\[
C=(I\cup G\cup O,W).
\]
\begin{itemize}
\item The nodes are the inputs (I), the gated (G) and the outputs of the circuit (O). They are labelled according to what they represent. 
\item The set of edges contains one edge for each wire (W). The edges are directed.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Truth assignments} 
A truth-assignment is a boolean vector that associates to the inputs of a circuit a boolean value to each input.

Under a truth-assignment each wire in the circuit will switch to the correct boolean value according to the gates traversed. The circuit commutes.
