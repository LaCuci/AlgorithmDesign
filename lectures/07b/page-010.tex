\subsection{Exercise: Complementary languages}
Let $L^c=\bits^*-L$. Prove that $\forall L\subseteq\bits^*$:
\[\underbrace{(L\in\classNPC)}_{\mathrm{HP1}}\land
\underbrace{(L^c\in\classNP)}_{\mathrm{HP2}}\Rightarrow
\underbrace{L^c\in\classNPC}_{\mathrm{TH}}.\]
