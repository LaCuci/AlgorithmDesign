\subsection{Exercise: The RAM halting problem}
Consider HALTING-PROBLEM (RAM version) (HP-RAM).

\begin{notesproblem}
\text{\textbf{I:}} & \langle C,x\rangle\text{, }C(x)\text{ RAM pseudocode with parameter }x\in\bits^*\text{.}\\
\text{\textbf{Q:}} & \text{Does }C(x)\text{ terminate?}
\end{notesproblem}

Prove that $\mathrm{HP\text{-}RAM}\in\classNPH$.
