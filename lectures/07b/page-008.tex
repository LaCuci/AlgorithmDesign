\sourceheading[Exercise: KNAPSACK]{EXERCISE}
Consider the following problem:

\textbf{KNAPSACK}

\textbf{I:} $\langle a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n,b;\ c_1,c_2,\ldots,c_n,K\rangle$,
\begin{description}[style=multiline,leftmargin=2em,labelwidth=1.5em]
\item[$a_i$:] weights of $n$ items;
\item[$b$:] total weight bound (capacity);
\item[$c_i$:] profit/value of the $n$ items;
\item[$K$:] lower bound on total profit ($K\in\mathbb N$).
\end{description}
\textbf{Q:} Is there a subset of items of total weight $\leq b$ and total profit $\geq K$?

Prove that $\mathrm{KNAPSACK}\in\classNPH$.
