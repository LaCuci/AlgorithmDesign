\subsection{Exercise: KNAPSACK}
Consider the following problem:

\textbf{KNAPSACK}

\begin{notesproblem}
\text{\textbf{I:}} & \langle a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n,b;\ c_1,c_2,\ldots,c_n,K\rangle\text{,}\\
& a_i\text{: weights of }n\text{ items;}\\
& b\text{: total weight bound (capacity); }\\
& c_i\text{: profit/value of the }n\text{ items;}\\
& K\text{: lower bound on total profit (}K\in\mathbb N\text{).}\\[4pt]
\text{\textbf{Q:}} & \text{Is there a subset of items of total weight }\leq b\\
& \text{and total profit }\geq K\text{?}
\end{notesproblem}

Prove that $\mathrm{KNAPSACK}\in\classNPH$.
