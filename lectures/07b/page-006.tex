\sourceheading[Exercise: Circuit testing]{EXERCISE}
Consider this decision problem:

\textbf{TESTING}

\begin{notesproblem}
\text{\textbf{I:}} & \langle C_1(x_1,\ldots,x_n),C_2(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\rangle\text{.}\\
\text{\textbf{Q:}} & \exists\vec b\in\bits^n:C_1(\vec b)\ne C_2(\vec b)\text{?}
\end{notesproblem}

Prove that $\mathrm{TESTING}\in\classNPH$.
