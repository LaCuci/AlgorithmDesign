\subsection{Inapproximability and Euclidean TSP}
\begin{notesresult}
\textbf{INAPPROXIMABILITY RESULT:}

For TRIANGLE-TSP there cannot exist a constant $\rho$-approximation algorithm for
\[\rho<\frac{123}{122}\approx1,008\]
unless $\classP=\classNP$ [Karpinski, Lampis, Schmied '15].
\end{notesresult}
\begin{notesresult}
Christofides' algorithm has been the best approximation algorithm for $\triangle$-TSP for 48 years (since 1976).

In 2020, a new (impractical) algorithm has been announced with
\[\rho\leq\frac32-10^{-36}\qquad\smile\]
[Karlin, Klein, Gharan '20].
\end{notesresult}
\begin{notesdefinition}
\textbf{FURTHER RESTRICTION: EUCLIDEAN TSP}

The nodes of $G$ are points $\in\mathbb R^d$ and
$c(u,v)=\lVert u-v\rVert_2=d_E(u,v)$.

There is a PTAS for EUCLIDEAN TSP.
Running time: $O(n\log n+2^{\operatorname{poly}(1/\eps)})$.
The algorithm is totally impractical.
\end{notesdefinition}
