\subsection{Tightness of the factor-3/2 bound}
It can be proved that the bound on $\rho(n)$ is tight, in the sense that there is a family of weighted complete graphs $G_n=(V_n,E_n),c_n$ ($|V_n|=n$)
\[\lim_{n\to+\infty}\rho_{G_n}(n)=\frac32.\]
