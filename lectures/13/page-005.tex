\section{The minimum set cover problem}
\subsection{Instances, coverings and applications}
An instance of SC is a pair $(X,\mathcal F)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $X$: universe of elements.
\item $\mathcal F$: family of subsets of $X$: $\mathcal F\subseteq\{S:S\subseteq X\}$ [$S\in\mathcal F\Rightarrow S\subseteq X$]. $\mathcal B(X)$: Boolean of $X$.
\end{itemize}
Covering constraint:
\[X=\bigcup_{S\in\mathcal F}S\qquad
(\forall x\in X\ \exists S\in\mathcal F:x\in S).\]
\begin{notesdefinition}
A subset $\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal F$ is a covering of $X$ (also: $\mathcal C$ covers $X$) if
$X=\bigcup_{S\in\mathcal C}S$.

(Observe that $\mathcal F$ covers $X$.)
\end{notesdefinition}
\begin{notesproblem}
&\text{We wish to find a minimum cardinality}\\
&\text{covering }\mathcal C^*\text{ of }X.
\end{notesproblem}
SC is an important problem which extends Vertex Cover.

APPLICATIONS:
\[X=\text{set of individuals},\qquad\mathcal F=\text{set of mailing lists}.\]
$|\mathcal C^*|$: minimum number of bulk e'mails to reach all individuals.
