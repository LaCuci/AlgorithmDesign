Since $|x|=|e(i)|=n$ ($i$: abstract instance), the independent parameter is the encoding length!

$\rightarrow$ ``Unreasonably'' lengthy encodings may ``mask'' the inefficiency of an algorithm!

\textbf{EXAMPLE:} Let $\mathcal I=\mathbb N-\{0\}$.

\textbf{BINARY ENCODING:} If $m\in\mathcal I=\mathbb N-\{0\}$,
\[
e_b(m)=\langle a_k,a_{k-1},\ldots,a_0\rangle,\quad a_k=1,
\]
with $k=\lfloor\log_2 m\rfloor$ and
\[
m=\sum_{j=0}^k a_j2^j.
\]
\textbf{NOTE 1:}
\[
|e_b(m)|=\lfloor\log_2 m\rfloor+1=\Theta(\log m).
\]
