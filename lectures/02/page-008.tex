Is GAUSS good or bad?
It's bad! For $m=2^{32}$ GAUSS executes $m\simeq4$ billion sums.

while

\begin{notesalgorithm}
\textbf{GAUSS2$(m)$}
\begin{notespseudocode}
\pseudocodeline{0}{\textbf{return} $m\cdot(m+1)/2$}
\end{notespseudocode}
\end{notesalgorithm}
executes 3 operations between numbers of size $\leq64$.

\textbf{MORALE:} The linear complexity under the unary encoding is MISLEADING. The fact that GAUSS is bad is masked by the sheer length of the unary encoding.

We will use only concise encodings whose length is comparable (i.e., at most polynomially larger) with a minimum-length encoding.
