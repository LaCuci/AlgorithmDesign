\subsection{A greedy vertex-cover counterexample}
Alternative greedy approach:
\begin{notesalgorithm}
\begin{notespseudocode}
\pseudocodeline{0}{$V'\gets\varnothing$; $E'\gets E$}
\pseudocodeline{0}{\textbf{repeat}}
\pseudocodeline{1}{select arbitrary edge $e=\{u,v\}$ \quad Greedy Choice}
\pseudocodeline{1}{$V'\gets V'\cup\{u\}$ \quad (only one endpoint)}
\pseudocodeline{1}{$E'\gets E'-\{e\in E':u\in e\}$ \quad Clean Up}
\pseudocodeline{0}{\textbf{until} $(E'=\varnothing)$}
\end{notespseudocode}
\end{notesalgorithm}
Algorithm correctly returns a VC $V'$ (all edges are covered when they are eliminated) but (counterexample):
\sourcefigure[0.48]{assets/10/page-005-wheel-graph.png}
The algorithm might select $s_1,s_2,\ldots,s_n$!
\[\Rightarrow\rho(|V|)\geq\frac{|V|-1}{1}=|V|-1.\]
