\subsection{Hardness of approximating CLIQUE}
[Feige et al. 1991] $\forall\varepsilon>0$ there cannot be a polynomial-time $O(|V|^{1-\varepsilon})$-approximation algorithm for MAXIMUM CLIQUE unless $\classP=\classNP$.

\textbf{MORALE:} CLIQUE is essentially inapproximable within any reasonable quality bound.
