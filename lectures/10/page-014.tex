\subsection{LP relaxation and lower bound}
Let us consider the continuous relaxation of ILP.

\textbf{LINEAR PROGRAM (LP)}
\begin{notesproblem}
&\min\sum_{u\in V}w(u)x_u\\
&\text{s.t.}\\
&x_u+x_v\geq1:\{u,v\}\in E\\
&0\leq x_u\leq1\qquad u\in V
\end{notesproblem}
Clearly, the optimal solution $x^*$ to LP is not necessarily integer and its cost is $\leq$ cost of the optimal integer solution.

\textbf{EXAMPLE:} $w(u)=1$ $\forall u\in V$.
\sourcefigure[0.9]{assets/10/page-014-ilp-lp-triangle.png}
We can use $x^*$ of LP to find a ``good'' solution $\widehat x$ to ILP via ROUNDING! Also, $C(x^*)$ lower bounds $C_{\mathrm{OPT}}$!
