We will often prove results under the hypothesis that $\classP\neq\classNP$ (or, equivalently, $\classP\cap\classNPC=\varnothing$).
\sourceheading{NP-COMPLETENESS PROOFS}
We have defined NPC but how do we place problems in NPC?

Given a candidate language $\bar L$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove $\bar L\in\classNP$.
\item Prove that $\forall L\in\classNP:\ L\polyred\bar L$.
\end{enumerate}
1. is usually easy (come up with a verification algorithm) (e.g., HAMILTON).

2. more difficult because of $\forall$! Infinite reductions!
