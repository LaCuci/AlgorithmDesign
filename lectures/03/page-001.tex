\section{Lesson 2 Recap}

Encodings: $\Pi_A\xrightarrow{\mathcal I\to e(\mathcal I)}\Pi_C:\bits^*\to\{0,1\}$.
We want to have reasonable/concise encodings.

Formal languages and decision problems are interchangeable:
\[
\underbrace{\Pi_C}_{\text{solution}}\longleftrightarrow
\underbrace{L_{\Pi_C}=\{x\in\bits^*:\Pi_C(x)=1\}}_{\text{decision}}.
\]
The solution of the decision problem corresponds to the decision of the formal language and vice versa.

Poly-time solution/decision is obtainable: the problem/language is in P.


Verification: algorithm $V(x,y)$.
\[
L_V=\{x\in\bits^*:\exists y\in\bits^*:\ V(x,y)=1\}.
\]
Poly-time verification algorithm exists: the problem/language is in NP.
Beware of the constraints : 
\begin{enumerate}
    \item poly-size certificate $[|y|=O(|x|^{k_1})]$;
    \item poly-time in both inputs $[T_V(x,y)=O((|x|+|y|)^{k_2})]$.
\end{enumerate}
