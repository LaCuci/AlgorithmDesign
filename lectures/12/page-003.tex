\subsection{Euler tours of the doubled tree}
\begin{notesresult}
\textbf{OBSERVATION (useful for later)}

The preorder visit on $T^*$ follows a nonsimple cycle (circuit) which uses each edge of $T^*$ twice:
\end{notesresult}
\sourcefigure[0.9]{assets/12/page-003-doubled-tree.png}
\[\mathrm{FPW}=\langle a,b,c,b,d,b,a,e,a\rangle\]
touches all edges of the multigraph once!

A nonsimple cycle touching ALL EDGES of a (multi-)graph ONCE is called EULER TOUR.
