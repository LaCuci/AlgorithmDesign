\textbf{CRUCIAL IDEA:} Let us complete $T^*$ into a Eulerian multisubgraph by adding $|V_{\mathrm{ODD}}|/2$ edges between pairs of nodes in $V_{\mathrm{ODD}}$ and use the resulting Euler TOUR instead of the FPW.

(with the new edges, all degrees become even)
\begin{notesdefinition}
\textbf{DEF} Given a weighted undirected complete graph $G'=(V',E')$ with $|V'|$ even, a perfect matching is a matching $M\subseteq E'$ of size $|V'|/2$ (a perfect matching ``touches'' all nodes in $V'$). Its cost is
\[c(M)=\sum_{\{u,v\}\in M}c(u,v).\]
\end{notesdefinition}
\begin{notesresult}
\textbf{FACT} A perfect matching $M^*$ of minimum cost can be found in $O(|V'|^3)$ time (blossom algorithm) (extremely complex).
\end{notesresult}
