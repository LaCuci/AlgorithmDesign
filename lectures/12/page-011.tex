\begin{notesresult}
\textbf{PROPERTY} Let $G=(V,E)$ be an arbitrary undirected graph, and let $V_{\mathrm{ODD}}$ be the subset of nodes of odd degree. Then $|V_{\mathrm{ODD}}|$ is even.
\end{notesresult}
\textbf{PROOF} In any graph, the sum of the degrees $\sum_{v\in V}\deg(v)=2|E|$ (each $e$ counted twice).

Then
\[\underbrace{2|E|}_{\text{EVEN}}=\sum_{v\in V}\deg(v)=\underbrace{\sum_{v\in V_{\mathrm{EVEN}}}\deg(v)}_{\text{EVEN}}+\underbrace{\sum_{v\in V_{\mathrm{ODD}}}\deg(v)}_{\text{it must be even!}}.\]
Since $\sum_{v\in V_{\mathrm{ODD}}}\deg(v)$ is even and the single terms are odd, the number of these terms $|V_{\mathrm{ODD}}|$ must be even!
