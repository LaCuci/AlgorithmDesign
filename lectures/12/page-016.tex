\subsection{The factor-3/2 approximation guarantee}
Putting it all together.

Let $\widehat\gamma=\mathrm{CHRISTOFIDES}(G=(V,E),c)$:
\begin{align*}
c(\widehat\gamma)&\leq c(E_{T^*})+c(M^*)\leq c(\gamma^*)+c(\gamma^*)/2\\
&=\frac32c(\gamma^*)
\end{align*}
\[\Rightarrow\rho=\frac{c(\widehat\gamma)}{c(\gamma^*)}\leq\frac32\frac{c(\gamma^*)}{c(\gamma^*)}=\frac32.\]
