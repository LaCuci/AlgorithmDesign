\textbf{Correctness:} the algorithm returns a tour $\widehat\gamma$ (since it shortcuts a Euler Tour $W$ of the Eulerian multigraph $G=(V,E_{T^*}\cup M^*)$).

\textbf{Running time:} $O(|V|^3)$ (dominated by M-C-MATCHING).

\textbf{Approximation ratio:} It suffices to bound $c(W)=c(E_{T^*})+c(M^*)$, since $c(\widehat\gamma)\leq c(W)$ (shortcutting). We already know that $c(E_{T^*})\leq c(\gamma^*)$.
