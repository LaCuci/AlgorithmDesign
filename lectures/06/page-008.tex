Let's prove that $f_{C\to\mathrm{VC}}$ is a reduction by logical manipulations of the definition of CLIQUE:
\[
x=\langle G=(V,E),k\rangle\in\mathrm{CLIQUE}
\]
\[
\Longleftrightarrow\exists V'\subseteq V,\ (|V'|=k)\land
\bigl(\forall u\neq v\in V:(u\in V')\land(v\in V')\Rightarrow\{u,v\}\in E\bigr).
\]
\{transform using contrapositive\} + \{$\neg(a\land b)=\neg a\lor\neg b$\}
\[
\Longleftrightarrow\exists V'\subseteq V,\ (|V'|=k)\land
\bigl(\forall u\neq v\in V':\{u,v\}\notin E\Rightarrow(u\notin V')\lor(v\notin V')\bigr).
\]
\[\{u,v\}\notin E\Longleftrightarrow\{u,v\}\in E^c,\qquad u\notin V'\Longleftrightarrow u\in V-V'.\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Longleftrightarrow\exists V'\subseteq V,\ (|V'|=k)\land\bigl(\forall u\neq v\in V':\{u,v\}\in E^c\Rightarrow{}& (u\in V-V')\\&\lor(v\in V-V')\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
\{now, set $V''=V-V':|V''|=|V|-k$\}
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Longleftrightarrow\exists V''\subseteq V,\ (|V''|=|V|-k)\land\bigl(\forall u\neq v\in V':\{u,v\}\in E^c\Rightarrow{}&(u\in V'')\\&\lor(v\in V'')\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
\{this says that $V''$ is a VC of $G^c$\}
\[\Longleftrightarrow f(x)=\langle G^c=(V,E^c),|V|-k\rangle\in\mathrm{VC}.\]
\textbf{REMARK:} The proof proceeds by logical equivalences (can be read both $\Downarrow$ and $\Uparrow$).
