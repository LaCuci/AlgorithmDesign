\[
f(\langle\phi(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m C_i\rangle)=\langle G_\phi,k_\phi\rangle,\qquad C_i=y_1^i\lor y_2^i\lor y_3^i.
\]
1. $k_\phi=m$.

2. $G_\phi=(V_\phi,E_\phi)$.
\[
V_\phi=\{v_j^i:1\leq i\leq m,1\leq j\leq3\}\Rightarrow|V_\phi|=3m.
\]
One node per literal occurrence.

$E_\phi$:
\[
\{v_{j_1}^{i_1},v_{j_2}^{i_2}\}\in E_\phi\Longleftrightarrow(i_1\neq i_2)\land(y_{j_1}^{i_1}\neq\neg y_{j_2}^{i_2}).
\]
Edges between literals of different clauses that can both be True under the same truth assignment.
