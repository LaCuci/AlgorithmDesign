With no loss of generality:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Assume that each variable $x_k$ of $\phi(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ appears in at least one clause (as $y_j^i=x_k$ or $y_j^i=\bar x_k$).
\item Assume that no clause contains $x_k$ and $\bar x_k$.
\end{enumerate}
Otherwise: Eliminate variable (1) or clause (2) ($x_k\lor\bar x_k=1$!).

We create:
\begin{itemize}
\item two numbers per variable $x_i$: $\delta_i,\delta'_i$, $1\leq i\leq n$ ($2n$).
\item two numbers per clause $C_j$: $s_j,s'_j$, $1\leq j\leq m$ ($2m$).
\end{itemize}
