Observe that the sum of all numbers yields
\[\underbrace{22\cdots2}_{n}\underbrace{66\cdots6}_{m}.\]
Two 1's due to $\delta_i$ and $\delta'_i$ in the $i$th column.

3 1's due to literals plus 1 and 2 due to $s_j$ \& $s'_j$ in the $j$th column.

No carry can ever be generated by summing any subset of numbers $\Rightarrow$ no ``interference'' between different columns.

The running time of the reduction is proportional to the number of digits generated:
\[
(\underbrace{2n}_{\delta_i,\delta'_i}+\underbrace{2m}_{s_j,s'_j}+\underbrace{1}_{t_\phi})\cdot(n+m)=O((n+m)^2)=O(|\langle\phi\rangle|^2).
\]
\textbf{NOTE:} After we generate the numbers in decimal, we can encode them in binary without changing the order of magnitude of the size of the encoding.
