\subsection{Polynomial running time}
\[\Rightarrow\underbrace{K}_{|L'_i|}<\frac{\ln t}{\ln(1+\eps/(2n))}+2.\]
However, for $|x|<1$: $\ln(1+x)\geq x/(1+x)$.
\begin{align*}
\Rightarrow |L'_i|=K
&\leq\frac{\ln t}{\frac\eps{2n}/(1+\frac\eps{2n})}+2\\
&\leq\frac{2n}\eps\underbrace{\left(1+\frac\eps{2n}\right)}_{\leq3/2}\ln t+2
=O\left(\frac n\eps\log t\right).
\end{align*}
Therefore
\[T_{\mathrm{ASS}}=O\left(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}|L'_i|\right)
=O\left(\frac{n^2\log t}\eps\right).\]
But $n=O(|\langle S,t\rangle|)$ and $\log t=O(|\langle S,t\rangle|)$.
Thus
\[T_{\mathrm{ASS}}(|\langle S,t\rangle|,\eps)=O\left(\frac{|\langle S,t\rangle|^3}\eps\right)\]
which is polynomial both in $|\langle S,t\rangle|$ and $1/\eps$.
