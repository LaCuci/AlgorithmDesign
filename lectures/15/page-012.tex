\begin{notesdefinition}
\textbf{DEFINITION}

The approximation ratio $\rho(i)$ of $A^R_\Pi(i)=S_A$ is defined as
\[\rho(i)=\max\left\{\frac{E(c(S_A))}{c(S^*)},\frac{c(S^*)}{E(c(S_A))}\right\}.\]
\end{notesdefinition}
