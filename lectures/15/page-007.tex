\subsection{The approximation guarantee}
Let now $y^*=c(S^*)=\sum_{s\in S^*}s$, $S^*\subseteq S=S_n$.
The lemma implies that $\exists z'\in L'_n$:
\[\frac{y^*}{(1+\eps/(2n))^n}\leq z'\leq y^*.\]
Therefore: $y^*/z'\leq(1+\eps/(2n))^n$.

OBSERVATION: We return MAX$(L'_n)\geq z'$!
Thus
\[\rho(n)=\frac{c(S^*)}{\mathrm{MAX}(L'_n)}\leq\frac{y^*}{z'}
\leq\left(1+\frac\eps{2n}\right)^n.\]
It suffices to show that $(1+\eps/(2n))^n\leq(1+\eps)$!
But:
\[\frac d{dn}\left(1+\frac\eps{2n}\right)^n>0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\lim_{n\to+\infty}\left(1+\frac\eps{2n}\right)^n=e^{\eps/2}.\]
