Replace each AND or OR gate with fan-in $\geq3$ with a cascade of gates with fan-in 2, e.g.,
\sourcefigure[0.9]{assets/05/page-007-gate-cascade.png}
The resulting circuit $C'$ is equivalent to $C$. Moreover $|\langle C'\rangle|=\Theta(|\langle C\rangle|)$ ($\#\text{ new gates}\leq\#\text{ wires of }C$).
\begin{itemize}
\item Next, we write the formula $\psi(\vec x,\vec y)\land y_0$ as done for formula satisfiability.
We have four types of subexpressions $\Gamma$:
\[
y_i\leftrightarrow w_i^1\lor w_i^2,\qquad y_i\leftrightarrow w_i^1\land w_i^2,\qquad y_i\leftrightarrow\bar w_i,\qquad y_0.
\]
($w_i$ either $x_k$ or $y_j$.)
\item We can re-write each subexpression as a conjunction of disjunctions:
\end{itemize}
\[
\begin{aligned}
y_i\leftrightarrow w_i^1\lor w_i^2
&\equiv(y_i\lor\bar w_i^1)\land(y_i\lor\bar w_i^2)\land(\bar y_i\lor w_i^1\lor w_i^2),\\
y_i\leftrightarrow w_i^1\land w_i^2
&\equiv(\bar y_i\lor w_i^1)\land(\bar y_i\lor w_i^2)\land(y_i\lor\bar w_i^1\lor\bar w_i^2),\\
y_i\leftrightarrow\bar w_i&\equiv(y_i\lor w_i)\land(\bar y_i\lor\bar w_i).
\end{aligned}
\]
