\sourceheading{PROBLEMS ON GRAPHS}
We will prove the NP-Completeness of three decision problems on graphs, related to very important optimization problems.

\textbf{DEF} Given $G=(V,E)$ undirected, a clique of size $k$ is a subset $V'\subseteq V$ of $k$ nodes such that each pair of distinct nodes in $V'$ is connected by an edge (clique: complete subgraph).
\[
V'\subseteq V:\ (|V'|=k)\land\bigl(\forall u\neq v\in V:(u\in V')\land(v\in V')\Rightarrow\{u,v\}\in E\bigr).
\]
