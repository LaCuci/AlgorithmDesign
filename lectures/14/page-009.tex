\subsection{Bounded-degree vertex cover and exercises}
OBSERVATIONS

The bound is much better when $|S_{\max}|$ is small.

APPLICATION: MINIMUM VERTEX COVER.
Restriction: graphs of degree 3: still hard!
Using the $\mathrm{VC}\polyred\mathrm{SC}$ reduction (which preserves the approximation) obtain
\[\rho_{3\text{-}\Delta\text{-VC}}=H(3)=1+\frac12+\frac13=\frac{11}{6}<2!\]
EXERCISES
\begin{enumerate}
\item Define $m_t=|U_t|$ ($m_0=|X|$, $\ldots$, $m_c=0$). Use the charging argument to prove that
\[|\mathcal C|=\sum_{x\in X}c_x\leq|\mathcal C^*|H(|X|).\]
(Hint: apply pigeon-hole to lowerbound $|S_t\cap U_t|$ as a function of $m_{t-1}$.)
\item Prove that VC on all graphs of degree $\leq2$ is in $\classP$.
\end{enumerate}
