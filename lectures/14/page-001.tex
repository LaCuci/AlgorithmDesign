\section{Recap: Greedy set cover}
SET-COVER: Given $\langle X,\mathcal F\rangle$, $\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal B(X)$ determine min-size covering $\mathcal C^*\subseteq\mathcal F$:
\[X=\bigcup_{S\in\mathcal C^*}S\quad(\text{size}=|\mathcal C^*|).\]
\begin{notesalgorithm}
\textbf{APPROX-SET-COVER ($X,\mathcal F$)}
\begin{notespseudocode}
\pseudocodeline{0}{$U\gets X;\quad\mathcal C\gets\varnothing$}
\pseudocodeline{0}{\textbf{while} $(U\neq\varnothing)$ \textbf{do}}
\pseudocodeline{1}{$S\gets\operatorname{argmax}\{|T\cap U|:T\in\mathcal F\}$}
\pseudocodeline{1}{$U\gets U-S;\quad\mathcal C\gets\mathcal C\cup\{S\}$}
\pseudocodeline{0}{\textbf{return} $\mathcal C$}
\end{notespseudocode}
\end{notesalgorithm}
We proved a lowerbound on the covering rate: Let $K=|\mathcal C^*|$:
\[\frac{|U_t-U_{t+1}|}{|U_t|}\geq\frac{|U_t|(1/K)}{|U_t|}=\frac1K.\]
$\therefore$ whence $\rho(n)\leq\lceil\ln n\rceil\leq1+\ln n$.
