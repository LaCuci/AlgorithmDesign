\begin{notesresult}
\[\sum_{x\in X}c_x=\sum_{t=1}^{|\mathcal C|}
\underbrace{\left(\sum_{x\in S_t\cap U_t}c_x\right)}_{1}=|\mathcal C|.\]
\end{notesresult}
We are ready to relate $\mathcal C^*$ and $\mathcal C$ using the weights.
\begin{notesresult}
\textbf{LEMMA}
\[\sum_{S\in\mathcal C^*}\sum_{x\in S}c_x\geq\sum_{x\in X}c_x=|\mathcal C|.\]
\end{notesresult}
PROOF: We know that $\mathcal C^*$ is a cover. Thus $\forall x\in X$, $c_x$ must be present as a summand in one of the sums on the l.h.s.!

ARGUMENT IDEA: We will prove that
\[\forall S\in\mathcal F:\quad\sum_{x\in S}c_x\leq H(|S|).\]
Therefore, from the lemma:
\[|\mathcal C|\leq\sum_{S\in\mathcal C^*}H(|S|).\]
