\subsection{Exponential running time}
RUNNING TIME: In the worst case:
\[\begin{cases}|L_i|=2|L_{i-1}|,\\|L_0|=1.\end{cases}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad |L_i|=2^i.\]
$\Rightarrow$ The time of MERGE-WD$(L_i,L_i+_t x_i)$ is $\Theta(2^i)$.
\[\Rightarrow T_{\mathrm{ESS}}(n)=\Theta(2^n)!\qquad n=|S|.\]
OBSERVATION: $2^{|S|}$ can be $\Theta(2^{|\langle S,t\rangle|})$.
