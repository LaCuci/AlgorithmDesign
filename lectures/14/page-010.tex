\section{An FPTAS for subset sum}
\subsection{The optimization problem and its decision version}
Optimization version of SS:
\begin{notesproblem}
\mathcal I&=\{\langle S,t\rangle:S\subseteq\mathbb N^+\text{ finite};\ t\in\mathbb N\}\\
\mathcal S&=\{T\subseteq\mathbb N^+:T\text{ finite}\}.
\end{notesproblem}
Given $i=\langle S,t\rangle$:
\[\mathcal S(i)=\left\{S'\subseteq S:c(S')=\sum_{s\in S'}s\leq t\right\}.\]
Type: MAX.
\begin{itemize}
\item Determine the subset of $S$ of maximum sum among all those (admissible) whose sum is not larger than target $t$.
\item ``Best lower approximation'' of $t$.
\end{itemize}
APPLICATION: $S=\{s_1,\ldots,s_n\}$ are weights and $t$ is the capacity of the container.
Aim: pack the container as much as possible (hypotheses: \textcircled{1} no volume constraints; \textcircled{2} all items have the same unit value).

HARDNESS: Decision version:
\begin{notesproblem}
&\text{SS'}\\
\text{I: }&\langle S,t,K\rangle\\
\text{Q: }&\exists S'\subseteq S:\quad\underbrace{K}_{\text{lower bound}}\leq\sum_{s\in S'}s\leq t\text{?}
\end{notesproblem}
